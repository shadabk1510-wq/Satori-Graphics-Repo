- **Paper** `#FEFBF2` (warm off-white), full frame, with a faint paper grain. This is one continuous background: the editorial world.
- **Ink** `#080707`: huge heavy lowercase grotesk headlines ("too far?"). Labels are bold sans ("Meta Muse").
- **Meta blue scribble M:** a thick round-capped stroke, blue gradient `#0064E0` → `#1A8CFF`. Built as a native shape path so it can **draw on** (Trim Paths) and **morph** (sketch → variations → final).
- **Alarm red** `#FD401F`: speech bubbles with "!", for anger and backlash beats.
- **People:** black-and-white grainy cut-outs (halftone feel) with a soft paper shadow.
- **Secondary (from the GIF):** lavender `#D9C3F0` with an ink-navy M pattern, for the logo-process section.
- **Motion:** the Satori house rules apply.
  - The hook is fast: whips, zoom-throughs, red bubbles popping in bursts.
  - The explanation is calm: clean pushes; every phrase or object lands on the VO word.
  - Every held frame drifts.
